# One observation per mastery step, backed by an explanation check

A subject moves up on a single observed success rather than two, because spaced reviews will contradict a lucky one soon enough and doubling the evidence cost would halve the programme's coverage. Independence stops at help level H1: any hint beyond a Socratic question makes the work `assisted`.

Because nothing prevents a learner from pasting another agent's answer, Techne asks for a short explanation when a correct solution appears with no intermediate attempts, far under the announced time, or using a technique never taught. A convincing explanation confirms `independent`; otherwise the subject stays `assisted` and an analogous exercise is scheduled. This is framed as "explain your choice here", never as an accusation.

## Consequences

- The placement test can grant `independent`: a cold success on an unseen task is strong evidence, stronger than one after a lesson.
- A failed lesson check grants nothing but blocks nothing; the exercise is what corrects the misunderstanding.
- Three failures on one subject mark it `blocked` and stop it for two weeks, rather than looping on it.
