# A morning red-thread project is where transfer is demonstrated

Isolated exercises prove a subject can be applied once, in a clean setting, which is not what the job asks for. Two mornings a week go to one Next.js + NestJS + database application, on a domain the learner picks in week 1, built across the twelve weeks and presentable at the end. A subject is first learned on an isolated exercise, then reinvested in the project one or two weeks later.

This makes the project, not the exercise, the evidence for the highest level of the mastery map ("Transféré"), and it is where survey subjects such as observability or performance are grafted onto real code.

## Consequences

- The morning has two shapes: isolated exercises and DSA on four mornings, project work on two; Techne schedules both and keeps the transfer lag deliberate.
- The learner finishes with two presentable artefacts, the morning application and the afternoon AI capstone, which serves the career goal ranked third.
